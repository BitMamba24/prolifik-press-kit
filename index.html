About Prolifik

Prolifik is an AI short-form video platform that turns a website URL, a prompt or a long video into branded vertical videos and auto-posts them to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Paste a website URL or write a prompt and Prolifik reads your homepage for logo, colours, tone of voice and positioning, then generates branded videos in six formats: Image Carousel, Text Story, Meme Reaction, Clip Montage, Video Hook and Demo, and Caption Meme. Every plan also includes an AI Clipper that turns YouTube videos, Twitch VODs and Kick VODs or an uploaded file into captioned vertical clips.

Nothing publishes on its own. Each video lands in the Library where you approve, skip, regenerate or edit it, and approved videos go to a calendar that posts through the official platform APIs. Output is watermark-free on every plan. See Prolifik pricing.
