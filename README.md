# Prolifik Press Kit

Logos, product facts and press contact for [Prolifik](https://getprolifik.com/), the AI short-form video platform.

Prolifik turns a website URL, a prompt or a long video into branded vertical videos in six formats and auto-posts them to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Every plan includes an AI Clipper for YouTube, Twitch and Kick VODs or uploaded files.

- Website: https://getprolifik.com/
- Pricing: https://getprolifik.com/pricing
- Press kit page: https://bitmamba24.github.io/prolifik-press-kit/
- Contact: info@getprolifik.com
